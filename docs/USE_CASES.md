# Legitimate use cases (society & security)

Smart Route is designed to support **ethical, authorized** scenarios where controlled location data matters.

## Mobile application QA

- Test ride-sharing, delivery, or fitness apps without physically driving routes  
- Reproduce edge cases (tunnel loss, speed jumps) with repeatable GPX exports  
- Validate geofence entry/exit logic in CI-adjacent manual test passes  

## Penetration testing & red team (with scope)

- Demonstrate geolocation trust issues in **client-owned** applications  
- Show how apps fail to detect mock providers when policy requires it  
- Produce evidence trails (audit log + GPX) for remediation reports  

## Privacy & security education

- University labs teaching how Android fuses GPS, network, and mock providers  
- Workshops on **consent**, data minimization, and location permission models  
- Comparing “app-level fake check-in” vs “OS-level mock GPS” attack surfaces  

## Journalism & human-rights research (controlled)

- Training advocates on **how location leaks occur** (not to falsify evidence)  
- Documenting surveillance risks when apps over-trust GPS without corroboration  

## What this tool is not for

- Stalking, impersonating presence to manipulate people, or romance scams  
- Bypassing employer/school/government controls without authorization  
- Fraud against banks, insurers, or gig platforms  

Violations may be illegal. Contributors and users are responsible for compliance with local law and institutional policies.
